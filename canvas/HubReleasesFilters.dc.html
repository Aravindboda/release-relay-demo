Open is the default

Open = still needs something: Submitted, Retrieving, Workflow running, Validating, Deploying, plus Checks failed and Ready for approval (waiting for the developer or an approver). Deployed, Failed (Failed, Deploy failed) and Rejected (Rejected, Closed) move to their own chips.

Raised by replaces Mine

A User lookup, set to you. Pick anyone, or clear it (✕) for everyone; the heading turns to Releases. Chip counts follow the Raised by user. Filtering runs on the server (listReleasesFor, up to 200, newest first, hotfixes first in Open).

Live, no reload

While the list shows an open release, it re-checks every 30 s: up to 5 running releases (least recently checked first, one by one), then the list and counts reload. Check now runs it straight away. The line disappears when nothing open is shown.
